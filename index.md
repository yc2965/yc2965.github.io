## About
I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences at Fudan University. I received my Ph.D. from Columbia University (advisor: Pierre Gentine) and conducted research at Harvard University.

My research has focused on advancing turbulence theory and modeling, investigating climate responses to man-made land surface heterogeneities, and identifying the key land–atmosphere processes controlling extreme events. By integrating theory, numerical simulations (DNS, LES, cloud-resolving models), field experiments, and machine learning ([DNNs](https://doi.org/10.1029/2021MS002847) and [generative AI](
https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2510.00383)), my goal is to provide more accurate predictions of extreme weather, air quality, and the impact of human activity on climate, and to strengthen resilience to extremes and promote sustainable land–climate management. 

My work has been recognized as an **Editors’ Suggestion** in *Physical Review Fluids* ([Cheng et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevFluids.8.114602)), selected **three times as a Research Spotlight** in AGU’s *EOS* ([Cheng et al. 2017](https://eos.org/research-spotlights/global-atmospheric-observations-may-need-tweaking-for-turbulence); [Cheng et al. 2024](https://eos.org/research-spotlights/seemingly-simple-climate-adaptation-strategy-could-backfire); [Cheng et al. 2026](https://eos.org/research-spotlights/how-much-will-western-wildfires-worsen-under-warming)), and featured in [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/news/2024/dec/05/weatherwatch-why-cooling-white-roofs-cause-neighbours-to-swelter).
